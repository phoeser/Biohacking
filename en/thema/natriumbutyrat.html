Sodium butyrate

Probiotics · butyrate, sodium salt of butyric acid, sodium butyrate, microencapsulated sodium butyrate

Butyrate is the short-chain fatty acid that gut bacteria produce from dietary fiber and on which the lining of the colon lives. Sodium butyrate delivers it as a capsule. There are several small randomized trials on it: in irritable bowel syndrome, ulcerative colitis and overweight with partly positive results, in high blood pressure with a rise in blood pressure. Whether the capsule replaces what dietary fiber does in the gut is an open question.

What sodium butyrate is

Sodium butyrate is the sodium salt of butyric acid, a short-chain fatty acid with four carbon atoms. In the body, butyrate is produced mainly in the colon when bacteria ferment dietary fiber; there it is an important energy source for the cells of the lining (Hamer 2008). Butyric acid itself smells and tastes unpleasant, so the salt is used for food supplements, often microencapsulated so that it is released further down in the gut.

Related approaches start earlier: prebiotics supply the food from which gut bacteria produce butyrate, among other things, and probiotics supply the bacteria themselves. In the BK-Score, human evidence stands at 4 out of 10 points. This axis rates the state of knowledge, not the substance: small positive studies are a start, not proof.

How it is supposed to work

In experiments, butyrate dampens inflammatory responses, strengthens the protective barrier of the gut lining and reduces oxidative stress. Inhibition of the inflammatory switch NF-kappa-B and inhibition of histone deacetylases are considered important mechanisms. A 2008 review emphasizes, however, that the effects depend heavily on concentration and model and that human data are limited (Hamer 2008).

For capsules, another question arises: where does the butyrate end up? In the Verhaar 2024 blood pressure study, butyrate levels rose in the blood but not in the stool. Testa 2026 points out that consumption in the gut and breakdown in the liver limit how much butyrate reaches the circulation. Butyrate from a capsule and butyrate that bacteria in the colon produce from dietary fiber therefore cannot simply be equated.

What is well supported

  • Butyrate as an energy source for the gut lining. Its role in the colon is well described.
  • Capsules raise butyrate levels in the blood. Two randomized trials showed this (Verhaar 2024, Testa 2026).
  • Individual positive findings in small RCTs. Small studies found benefits in irritable bowel syndrome, in ulcerative colitis as add-on therapy and in obesity. Confirmation in larger studies is still pending.

What the studies show

Irritable bowel syndrome: individual symptoms less frequent

Banasiewicz 2013 gave 66 patients with irritable bowel syndrome, who had been receiving standard therapy for at least three months, additional microencapsulated sodium butyrate or placebo. After 4 weeks, pain during bowel movements occurred less often, and after 12 weeks so did urgency and irregular bowel habits. Abdominal pain, bloating and defecation disorders did not improve significantly. The authors summarize: individual symptoms occurred less often but did not become markedly weaker.

Ulcerative colitis: as add-on therapy

Karłowicz 2025 treated 98 adults with mild to moderate ulcerative colitis for eight weeks with additional 2 × 300 mg microencapsulated sodium butyrate or placebo. In the butyrate group, 51 percent improved clinically, 31.4 percent achieved clinical and 42.2 percent biochemical remission; the authors report these results as significant. The abstract does not give the rates for the placebo group, and the study is small and short.

Weight: children and adults

Coppola 2022 randomized 54 children and adolescents with obesity to sodium butyrate (20 mg per kilogram of body weight daily) or placebo, in addition to standard treatment over 6 months. The primary endpoint, a BMI reduction of at least 0.25 standard deviations, was reached by 96 percent with butyrate and 56 percent with placebo. Testa 2026 gave 46 overweight adults 1,875 mg daily or placebo for 12 weeks on the same diet. Without diabetes, they lost 7.0 kg with butyrate and 3.2 kg with placebo; with type 2 diabetes, weight did not differ, but triglycerides fell.

Type 2 diabetes with irritable bowel symptoms

Panufnik 2026 gave 52 patients with type 2 diabetes and irritable bowel symptoms 1.5 g microencapsulated sodium butyrate daily or placebo for 12 weeks. In the butyrate group, abdominal pain, diarrhea and bloating decreased, as did weight, HbA1c and the HOMA index; in the placebo group nothing changed significantly. The analysis was mainly within groups, not a direct comparison, and the study was registered only retrospectively.

High blood pressure: blood pressure rose

Verhaar 2024 tested sodium butyrate in 23 people with high blood pressure whose blood pressure medication was stopped for the study; the placebo group received a corresponding amount of table salt. After 4 weeks, daytime blood pressure with butyrate was 9.6 mmHg systolic and 5.1 mmHg diastolic higher than with placebo, even though butyrate had lowered blood pressure in animal experiments. The authors advise caution in further butyrate studies.

Where the data stop

  • Substitute for dietary fiber. Whether butyrate from a capsule achieves what dietary fiber does in the colon has not been shown; in the blood pressure study, butyrate content in the stool did not rise.
  • Gut health in healthy people. Butyrate is often advertised for this, including against a leaky gut. The studies cited here concern people with bowel or metabolic diseases; a benefit for healthy people has not been demonstrated.
  • Long term. The longest study lasted 6 months, most 4 to 12 weeks.
  • Size of the studies. All randomized trials cited here had fewer than 100 participants and used different products and amounts.

Status, approval and legal

In Germany, sodium butyrate is sold as a food supplement; it is not a medicine. Sodium butyrate has no entry in the European Commission’s Novel Food Catalogue. For tributyrin, a related butyrate source made of glycerol and three butyric acid residues, a consultation procedure in Poland in 2024 concluded that it is a novel food; it is not authorized. An explicit official classification of sodium butyrate as a food supplement could not be found. There is no authorized health claim for butyrate.

Safety

The data come from studies lasting 4 weeks to 6 months, each with fewer than 100 participants. The most notable signal is the rise in blood pressure in people with high blood pressure. Sodium butyrate consists of about one fifth sodium, which counts on a low-sodium diet. With high blood pressure, butyrate should therefore be discussed with a physician. Frequently asked questions about sodium butyrate

Does sodium butyrate work for irritable bowel syndrome?

A randomized trial with 66 patients found that pain during bowel movements occurred less often with microencapsulated sodium butyrate, and after 12 weeks there was less urgency. Abdominal pain and bloating did not improve significantly, and overall the symptoms did not become markedly weaker. This is a small finding that needs larger confirmatory studies.

Is butyrate from capsules the same as butyrate from dietary fiber?

Not necessarily. Butyrate from dietary fiber is produced directly in the colon, where the lining uses it. From capsules, part of it is apparently absorbed early: in one study, butyrate levels rose in the blood but not in the stool. Microencapsulation is meant to change this; how much of it reaches the colon was not measured in the studies cited here.

Does sodium butyrate help with weight loss?

Two small studies found benefits: children with obesity lowered their BMI more often, and overweight adults without diabetes lost 7.0 instead of 3.2 kg on the same diet. In adults with type 2 diabetes there was no difference in weight. The studies had 54 and 46 participants and lasted 6 months and 12 weeks respectively.

Can sodium butyrate raise blood pressure?

In a randomized trial with 23 people with high blood pressure, daytime blood pressure rose by 9.6 mmHg systolic compared with placebo after 4 weeks, even though butyrate had lowered blood pressure in animal experiments. The placebo group received a corresponding amount of table salt. With high blood pressure, butyrate should therefore be discussed with a physician.

Is sodium butyrate permitted in Germany?

Sodium butyrate is sold as a food supplement and has no entry in the EU Novel Food Catalogue. The related butyrate source tributyrin was classified as a novel food in 2024 and is not authorized. There is no authorized health claim for butyrate.
