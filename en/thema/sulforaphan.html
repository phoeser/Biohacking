Sulforaphane

Antioxidant · Broccoli sprout extract

Sulforaphane from broccoli sprouts is one of the best-studied plant compounds when it comes to the body’s own detoxification. It has been shown in humans that it switches on detoxification enzymes and that pollutants are excreted faster. The data on autism are surprisingly clear; those on cancer prevention and blood glucose are considerably thinner.

In short

Sulforaphane is formed from glucoraphanin when broccoli or broccoli sprouts are chewed, and it activates the transcription factor Nrf2, which controls protective and detoxification genes. In a randomized trial with 291 people in China, the excretion of benzene breakdown products rose by 61 % with a sprout beverage. Two meta-analyses, each of 6 randomized trials, show an improvement in social symptoms in autism after 4 to 10 weeks. The catch: for cancer prevention there are only biomarkers in humans, the autism effect is no longer significant after 18 weeks, and the quality of broccoli supplements varies widely.

What sulforaphane is

Sulforaphane is an isothiocyanate, that is, a mustard oil. In the plant it is present as the inactive precursor glucoraphanin. Only when cells are damaged, for example during chewing, does glucoraphanin meet the plant’s own enzyme myrosinase and become sulforaphane. Young broccoli sprouts contain particularly large amounts of glucoraphanin.

Cooking destroys myrosinase. Gut bacteria then take over part of the conversion, but less reliably. How much difference this makes is shown by a small study with 12 adults: after 200 g of cooked broccoli with 1 g of mustard powder, 44.7 µmol of a sulforaphane breakdown product per gram of creatinine were found in the urine, without mustard powder only 9.8. Mustard seed itself contains myrosinase and steps in for the destroyed enzyme.

How it is supposed to work

Sulforaphane activates the transcription factor Nrf2. This switches on a program of protective genes, including phase II detoxification enzymes that couple pollutants to glutathione and make them excretable. Unlike with many plant compounds, this step can be measured directly in humans.

That is exactly what the studies in Qidong did, a region with high air pollution. They measured how much glutathione-conjugated benzene and acrolein ends up in the urine. A follow-up study with 170 participants also showed a dose-response relationship: only the full dose significantly increased benzene excretion, by 63.2 %, the half and one-fifth doses did not. From this, the authors derive an effective amount of about 25 µmol of excreted sulforaphane metabolites per day. Important in practice: bioavailability did not decrease over 12 weeks of daily intake, so no habituation was seen. For blood glucose there is a separate mechanism: in liver cells, sulforaphane lowered glucose production via Nrf2. For autism, anti-inflammatory and mitochondrial effects are also discussed.

User reports

After the first autism study, the researchers followed up with the families until 2016. 16 of 26 families in the sulforaphane group responded; 9 of the young men were still taking a sulforaphane supplement, and one more person was planning to. The reports of the caregivers were published, many of whom describe positive changes. This is an uncontrolled case series and not proof of efficacy, but it shows how those affected experienced the effect themselves.

What matters in a product

The Institute of Pharmaceutical Biology at the University of Münster analyzed 14 broccoli food supplements and found very large differences in quality. In several products, glucoraphanin was not detectable at all. Then there is the question of conversion: if a supplement also supplies active myrosinase, sulforaphane was 3 to 4 times more bioavailable in one study. In a more recent crossover study with 16 people, mustard seeds raised bioavailability from 18.6 to 39.8 %.

What is well supported

Best supported is the activation of the body’s own detoxification in humans. In a randomized trial with 291 participants, the excretion of the glutathione conjugates of benzene rose by 61 % and of acrolein by 23 % over 12 weeks, and the effect did not wane over the entire period.

The second strong line is autism. A meta-analysis of 6 randomized trials with 333 participants found a standardized mean difference of -0.98 on the Social Responsiveness Scale after 4 to 5 weeks and of -1.11 after 8 to 10 weeks, that is, large effects. A second meta-analysis sees improvements in overall symptoms and social interaction. Adverse events did not occur more often with sulforaphane than with placebo.

What the studies show

Air pollutants in Qidong

Randomized, placebo-controlled, 291 participants, 12 weeks. The sprout beverage supplied 600 µmol of glucoraphanin and 40 µmol of sulforaphane daily. Excretion of the benzene conjugates rose by 61 %, that of the acrolein conjugates by 23 %; crotonaldehyde remained unchanged. This is a pharmacological endpoint; whether it prevents diseases was not studied.

The first autism study

Double-blind, 29 young men aged 13 to 27 on sulforaphane and 15 on placebo, 50 to 150 µmol daily depending on body weight over 18 weeks. Scores on the Aberrant Behavior Checklist fell by 34 %, those on the Social Responsiveness Scale by 17 %; with placebo they changed by less than 3.3 %. After discontinuation, the scores rose back toward baseline.

Prediabetes and gut flora

Double-blind, 35 people with broccoli sprout extract and 39 with placebo over 12 weeks. The predefined target of 0.3 mmol/l lower fasting blood glucose was missed; overall the value fell by 0.2 mmol/l. A subgroup with a particular metabolic profile responded more strongly, with 0.4 mmol/l, and these people more often had gut bacteria with a gene needed for the conversion of glucoraphanin.

Where the data stop

For cancer prevention there are no clinical endpoints in humans. A systematic review of 8 randomized trials in cancer patients found altered biomarkers and inconsistent PSA values; a meta-analysis was not possible because of the differences between the trials. The protective effects come from laboratory and animal experiments; a systematic review of 9 animal studies found, for example, fewer skin tumors after carcinogen and UVB exposure.

In autism, the effect does not hold in the meta-analysis: after 18, 22 and 36 weeks the difference was no longer significant, and heterogeneity was high. In schizophrenia, sulforaphane did not improve overall symptoms in 4 studies with 369 patients. Negative symptoms improved slightly after 12 weeks, by 1.06 points, but the effect did not last; at least fewer patients discontinued treatment with sulforaphane. For blood glucose, the prediabetes study missed its primary target, even though an older study in poorly controlled type 2 diabetes and overweight found lower fasting values and HbA1c. Statements about the liver as a detoxification organ are based on excretion markers, not on liver values.

Status, approval and legal

Broccoli sprouts, powders and extracts are foods or food supplements in Germany. Health claims on broccoli and sulforaphane, for example on cell protection or gastrointestinal health, have not been authorized by the EU after review by EFSA. There is no legal maximum amount, and the contents of supplements vary widely. Doping relevance is not described in the literature reviewed.

Safety

In the autism studies, irritability, insomnia, constipation and headaches occurred, but not more often than with placebo: irritability, for example, in 10.0 % of the 180 participants on sulforaphane and in 8.5 % of the 153 in the control groups. In the prediabetes study there were gastrointestinal complaints; there were no serious side effects. In the first, high-dose autism study, 2 participants each had one seizure; anyone with epilepsy should discuss this with a doctor. The frequently cited concern about the thyroid was not confirmed in a study with 45 women over 84 days; TSH, thyroid hormone and autoimmune status remained the same. There is no official upper limit.

Frequently asked questions about sulforaphane

What does sulforaphane do?

It has been established in humans that it activates detoxification enzymes and that pollutants such as benzene are excreted faster. In autism, two meta-analyses show improvements in social symptoms. For cancer prevention there are only laboratory and animal data as well as biomarkers.

How much sulforaphane was used in studies?

The large detoxification study gave 600 µmol of glucoraphanin and 40 µmol of sulforaphane daily. In autism studies, the amount ranged between 50 and 150 µmol depending on body weight. A follow-up study found an effect only from about 25 µmol of excreted metabolites per day.

Are broccoli sprouts better than capsules?

Fresh sprouts bring their own myrosinase, so sulforaphane is formed well. With capsules, quality varies widely; in an analysis of 14 products, several contained no detectable glucoraphanin. Supplements with active myrosinase performed better in studies.

Why should you eat mustard with broccoli?

Cooking destroys the enzyme myrosinase, which releases sulforaphane. Mustard seed contains this enzyme. In one study, the yield with 1 g of mustard powder added to cooked broccoli was more than 4 times as high.

Does sulforaphane harm the thyroid?

In a study with 45 women over 84 days, thyroid values and autoimmune status remained unchanged. Longer-term data are lacking. Anyone with a thyroid disease should discuss high amounts with a doctor.

Does sulforaphane help with autism?

Two meta-analyses, each of 6 studies, show improvements in social symptoms after 4 to 10 weeks. After 18 weeks the difference was no longer significant. Medical supervision is important, also because of isolated seizures in one study.
