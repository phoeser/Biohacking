WADA list (Prohibited List)

In short

The WADA list is the Prohibited List of the World Anti-Doping Agency: it sets out which substances and methods are banned in sport. It is updated every year and takes effect on 1 January.

It distinguishes between substances that are prohibited at all times, such as anabolic agents, peptide hormones, growth factors and SARMs, and those prohibited only in competition, such as many stimulants. A separate class also prohibits all substances that are not approved for human use anywhere. Many entries expressly include substances with a similar structure or effect, even if they are not named.

The list applies to athletes who compete under anti-doping rules, not to the general public. In Germany, the Anti-Doping Act additionally makes trafficking and possession of more than small quantities of certain substances for doping purposes a criminal offence.

An entry says nothing about whether a substance works — only that it is not allowed in sport.

Banned in sport does not mean: effective.

Where this comes up in the fact checks

  • BPC-157 – Prohibited in competitive sport, even though there is no completed phase II trial.
  • Ostarine – Prohibited at all times under the WADA list and named in the annex of the German Anti-Doping Act.
  • Clenbuterol – A prescription asthma drug in Germany, prohibited in sport at all times.
