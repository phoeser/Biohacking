Questions & complaints

Not everyone is looking for an active substance. Here it starts with the question: a direct answer, what matters first, when a medical check-up makes sense, and how well supported the topics in the database are.

  • Losing belly fat – what has been shown to work?Losing belly fat: why sit-ups are not enough, which training reduces deep belly fat the most, and what sleep, fasting and weight-loss injections can do.
  • Cravings and blood sugar swings – what helps?Why cravings come after eating and what smooths the curve: fiber, protein, walking after meals, sleep, vinegar and glucose sensors.
  • Lowering silent inflammation – what is proven?What lies behind silent inflammation, what the CRP value tells you and what demonstrably lowers it: exercise, weight loss, gums, sleep, omega-3 and turmeric.
  • Joint pain and stiff joints – what helps?Joint pain and stiff joints: why exercise is the most effective remedy, what losing weight achieves and what turmeric, collagen, glucosamine and red light can do.
  • Hair loss – what demonstrably helps?Hereditary, stress-related or a deficiency? What demonstrably works for hair loss: minoxidil, finasteride, red light, PRP, and which hair vitamins you can skip.
  • Slowing skin aging – what is proven?Slowing skin aging: why daily sun protection is the best-supported measure, what retinol can do and what collagen, astaxanthin, hyaluronic acid and red light achieve.
  • Blood pressure and heart – what can I do myself?Measuring correctly, less salt, more potassium, training, less alcohol, sleep apnea: what demonstrably lowers your blood pressure and when medication belongs in the picture.
  • Strengthening the immune system – what really makes a difference?Sleep, exercise, vaccinations, zinc, vitamin D and C, probiotics: what demonstrably supports your immune system and where the studies disappoint.
  • Poor concentration and brain fog – what helps?Brain fog and poor concentration: why sleep and exercise come first, which blood values matter and what caffeine, L-theanine, creatine and bacopa can do.
  • Low desire, low drive – is it testosterone?When is testosterone behind low desire and drive? How to have it measured properly, what sleep and weight can do and what boosters can really achieve.
  • Longevity: Where do I start?Longevity for beginners: why fitness, strength, blood pressure and sleep come before any pill, which values you should know and where supplements stand today.
  • Building and keeping muscle after 40 – what matters?Building muscle after 40: why strength training is the most important lever, how much protein the studies support and what creatine, whey and hormones really contribute.
  • Recovering faster after training – what helps?Sleep, protein, massage, ice bath, sauna, creatine and HRV: what really speeds up recovery after training and what tends to slow down your progress.
  • I sleep badly – what really helps?Slept badly, again? What matters first, why behavioral therapy comes before pills, and what magnesium, L-theanine, glycine and melatonin can do.
  • I'm always tired – what helps?Always tired? What matters first – sleep, blood values, exercise, light – and what iron, vitamin B12, caffeine and rhodiola really deliver according to studies.
  • Constant stress and inner restlessness – what helps?Constant stress and inner restlessness: what exercise, breathing exercises and meditation have been shown to do and what ashwagandha, L-theanine and rhodiola can offer.
  • Bloating and digestive problems – what helps?Bloating, abdominal pain, changing stools: what low-FODMAP, psyllium, exercise, probiotics, peppermint oil and gut tests have been shown to do.
  • Menopausal symptoms – what is supported by evidence?Hot flashes, sleep, mood, bones: what hormone therapy, hormone-free medications, strength training and supplements have been shown to do during menopause.
