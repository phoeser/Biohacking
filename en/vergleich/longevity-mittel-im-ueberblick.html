Longevity supplements and drugs 2026: what is proven in humans

21 substances from metformin to NMN: what is approved, what has been shown in humans and where the data stop.

By Paul Höser · 6 min read · updated November 15, 2026

The short answer

The best-studied are approved medicines such as metformin, acarbose, rapamycin and canagliflozin: they are well supported for their actual diseases, and they extended lifespan in mice. Among supplements, NMN and NR reliably raise NAD+ levels. That any of these slows ageing or extends life in humans has not been shown so far, and resveratrol did not hold up in trials. The best human evidence is for unspectacular things such as exercise, muscle strength and sleep (as of 15 November 2026).

How to read the table

  • Mice are not humans. Many longevity promises come from the US Interventions Testing Program (ITP), in which mice lived longer. No completed trial has yet tested whether this works the same in humans.
  • A lab value is not a benefit. NMN and NR reliably raise NAD+ in blood. Whether that makes you age more healthily is a different question, and it is open.
  • The BK-Score rates the state of knowledge. "Well supported, heavily overhyped" for metformin means: the diabetes data are solid, but the anti-ageing promise goes far beyond them.
  • Medicines belong in medical hands. For drugs used off-label we give no amounts.

What has been shown to work

The strongest human data exist for things that cost little or nothing: regular exercise and strength training, enough sleep, not smoking, little alcohol and a plant-based diet, plus treating high blood pressure and raised blood lipids. A starting point is Longevity: Where do I start?.

Frequently asked questions

Which longevity supplement has the best evidence?

None has been shown to extend life in humans. The most solid lab data exist for NR and NMN, which reliably raise NAD+ levels, and for urolithin A with small randomised trials on mitochondria and muscle. A benefit for health or lifespan has not been shown for any of them.

Does NMN do anything?

NMN raises NAD+ levels in the blood. Whether this leads to healthier ageing has not been shown. EFSA assessed beta-NMN in 2026 for safety only, not for efficacy; it is not yet authorised in the EU.

Is metformin an anti-ageing drug?

Metformin is a well-studied diabetes drug. Whether it slows ageing in healthy people is what the planned TAME trial is meant to find out; there are no results yet. For healthy people it is prescription-only and off-label.

What has been shown to help you live longer?

The best human evidence is for lifestyle factors: regular exercise, muscle strength, enough sleep, not smoking and a plant-based diet, plus treating high blood pressure and raised blood lipids.
