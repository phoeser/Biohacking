Weight-loss drugs 2026 compared: injections and pills

19 drugs from Wegovy to retatrutide: what is approved, what is in trials and what the data show.

By Paul Höser · 5 min read · updated November 14, 2026

The short answer

Four weight-loss medicines are approved in the EU: semaglutide (Wegovy, also as a tablet since September 2026), tirzepatide (Mounjaro), liraglutide (Saxenda) and orlistat. Among the approved drugs, tirzepatide shows the largest weight loss: up to 20.9 percent after 72 weeks, and more than semaglutide in the head-to-head SURMOUNT-5 trial. Even larger numbers come from drugs still in trials: retatrutide reached 25 percent in TRIUMPH-1. The first non-peptide GLP-1 pill, orforglipron, is approved in the US and UK and recommended in the EU, but not yet available there (as of 14 November 2026).

How to read the table

  • The percentages are not directly comparable. A 13-week trial measures something different from an 80-week trial, and people with type 2 diabetes usually lose less weight on the same drugs.
  • Phase 2 numbers are often higher than later phase 3 results. Small trials with selected participants tend to overestimate the effect.
  • The BK-Score rates the state of knowledge, not the effect. "Well supported, heavily overhyped" means the data are solid, but the promises online go further.
  • All of these drugs are prescription-only or not approved at all. Whether one is suitable is a medical decision. In Germany, statutory health insurance does not pay for weight loss (§ 34 SGB V).

Caution when buying online

Unapproved drugs such as retatrutide are not available as tested medicines outside clinical trials. What is sold online as "GLP-3" or "reta" is untested material with unclear contents; the trial results do not apply to it. Counterfeits of approved drugs are also in circulation.

Frequently asked questions

Which weight-loss injection works best?

Among the drugs approved in the EU, tirzepatide (Mounjaro) shows the largest weight loss: up to 20.9 percent after 72 weeks in SURMOUNT-1, and 20.2 versus 13.7 percent with semaglutide in the head-to-head SURMOUNT-5 trial. Retatrutide, not yet approved, reached 25.0 percent in TRIUMPH-1.

Which weight-loss pills are there?

In Germany, the Wegovy tablet (semaglutide) has been available since 1 September 2026, alongside orlistat. Orforglipron (Foundayo) is approved in the US and UK; in the EU, the CHMP recommended approval on 15 October 2026, and the European Commission decision was still pending on 13 November 2026.

Does health insurance pay for weight-loss injections in Germany?

Not for weight loss: medicines for weight reduction are excluded from reimbursement under § 34(1) SGB V. For type 2 diabetes, however, GLP-1 drugs such as Ozempic or Mounjaro can be prescribed and reimbursed.

What is the difference between GLP-1, GIP and amylin?

All three are the body's own satiety and metabolic hormones. GLP-1 drugs such as semaglutide act on one receptor, tirzepatide also on the GIP receptor, retatrutide on three receptors. Amylin drugs such as eloralintide and petrelintide use a second satiety system and are also combined with GLP-1, for example in CagriSema.
