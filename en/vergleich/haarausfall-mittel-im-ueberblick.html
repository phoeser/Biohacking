Hair loss treatments compared: what is proven in humans

13 treatments and procedures for hereditary hair loss: approval, what has been shown on hair and BK-Score at a glance.

By Paul Höser · 6 min read · updated November 15, 2026

The short answer

The best evidence is for the two treatments approved in Germany for hereditary hair loss: finasteride and minoxidil. For both, the effect has been counted in blinded trials: they slow hair loss, and additional hair grows back. Dutasteride did even slightly better than finasteride in trials but is not approved for hair loss in Germany. New substances meant to block the hormone signal only at the scalp are in phase 3, with figures so far only from the manufacturers. For biotin with normal intake, saw palmetto, GHK-Cu or exosomes, solid data on scalp hair are lacking (as of 15 November 2026).

How to read the table

  • This is about hereditary hair loss. Alopecia areata and hair loss due to iron deficiency, thyroid problems or after an illness have other causes and should be checked by a doctor.
  • Slowing down is already a success. Without treatment, hereditary hair loss keeps progressing; in the finasteride trials, loss continued on placebo and was slowed on the drug.
  • Manufacturer data are a start. For pyrilutamide and clascoterone, the results so far come from company announcements, not peer-reviewed publications.
  • No doses, no sources. For unapproved substances we deliberately give no amounts and link no sellers.

Side effects worth knowing

  • Finasteride: sexual dysfunction is known. Since a Dear Healthcare Professional letter of 15 September 2025, suicidal thoughts are also an established side effect of the tablets; with depressed mood, treatment should be stopped and medical advice sought.
  • Dutasteride: in the trial in men with hair loss, sexual side effects occurred twice as often as on placebo, 16 versus 8 percent. It must not be used in women, children or adolescents.
  • Minoxidil: on the scalp, itching and irritation are most common. With the low-dose tablet, a large analysis found increased body hair most often (15.1 percent), less often dizziness, fluid retention and palpitations; pericardial effusion and fluid retention are known from blood pressure treatment.
  • RU58841: after application to the scalp the substance reaches the blood, and breakdown products remain in the body for days. There are no tolerability data from human trials.

When to see a doctor

Sudden, patchy or very heavy hair loss, hair loss in women or hair loss together with other symptoms should be checked by a doctor before reaching for any product. Finasteride, dutasteride and minoxidil tablets are prescription-only and belong in medical hands.

Frequently asked questions

What works best against hereditary hair loss?

The best evidence is for finasteride and minoxidil, which are approved in Germany for hereditary hair loss. Both slow hair loss, and in blinded trials additional hair grows back. Dutasteride worked slightly better in trials but is not approved for hair loss in Germany.

Is RU58841 an alternative to finasteride?

Not shown so far. RU58841 worked in animals and in scalp tissue, but there is no study of its effect in humans. It has never been approved, and after application it reaches the blood. In sport it is banned as a non-approved substance.

Does biotin help against hair loss?

Only in true biotin deficiency. With normal intake there is no positive controlled trial on hair growth. High amounts of biotin can also distort lab results, which should be mentioned before blood tests.

What side effects does finasteride have?

Sexual dysfunction is known. Since 2025, suicidal thoughts have been confirmed as a side effect of the tablets; their frequency is unknown. Anyone who notices depressed mood on finasteride should stop treatment and seek medical advice.
