Anabolic steroids and SARMs: evidence, risks, legal status

29 steroids, SARMs and products sold as SARMs: approval, documented risks and BK-Score at a glance.

9 min read · updated November 15, 2026

The short answer

Anabolic steroids work, and that is well shown in humans: more muscle mass and more strength, considerably more with training. The price is now measured just as well. In a Danish registry cohort, users had a 2.81-fold risk of death compared with men of the same age and an 8.90-fold risk of heart muscle disease, and in some men their own testosterone production does not fully recover even years later (anabolic steroids). For most individual steroids, all SARMs and the products sold as SARMs there are hardly any controlled human data; what is documented is mainly liver injury. In Germany, only testosterone is on the market as a medicine, prescribed for confirmed deficiency; a few others are approved in neighbouring countries or Japan. In sport, everything here is banned at all times (as of 15 November 2026).

How to read the table

  • "Hardly any data" does not mean "harmless". For trenbolone, boldenone or most SARMs there are no studies because they were never developed for humans or were stopped early. What exists are case reports, and they almost always concern the liver, the heart or mental health.
  • 17α-alkylated tablets strain the liver the most. These include Dianabol, Anavar, Anadrol, Winstrol, Halotestin, Oral Turinabol and the designer steroids. Cholestasis, blood-filled cavities in the liver and liver tumours are described for this group.
  • The hormone axis shuts down with all of them. Supplied androgens and SARMs suppress the body's own testosterone and sperm production. That is not a rare side effect but the direct consequence of how they work.
  • No doses, no sources, no cycle plans. For unapproved substances we deliberately give no amounts and link no sellers.

The legal situation in Germany

The steroids and SARMs on this list are covered by the German Anti-Doping Act, either by name or through their substance class. Manufacturing, trafficking and supplying them for doping are prohibited and punishable, as are acquiring, possessing and bringing them into Germany in non-small quantities for doping in sport (§ 2, § 4). Unapproved finished medicines may not be placed on the market (§ 21 German Medicines Act); the approved substances are prescription-only. In competitive sport all substances listed here are banned at all times under the WADA Prohibited List, steroids and SARMs under S1, cardarine, GW-0742 and SR9009 as metabolic modulators under S4. This is not legal advice.

Grey market and "research chemicals"

SARMs and designer steroids are often sold as research chemicals "not for human consumption" or as supplements. The US Food and Drug Administration makes clear that SARMs are not dietary supplements but unapproved drugs. Content and purity of such products are not tested. Anyone who notices jaundice, dark urine, itching, chest pain or palpitations should have it checked by a doctor and mention the use openly.

Frequently asked questions

Are anabolic steroids legal in Germany?

Approved anabolic steroids such as testosterone are available only on prescription. Under the German Anti-Doping Act, manufacturing, trafficking and supplying them for doping are punishable, as are acquiring and possessing non-small quantities for doping in sport. Unapproved finished medicines may not be placed on the market. In sport, all are banned at all times. This is not legal advice.

Are SARMs safer than steroids?

That has not been shown. SARMs were meant to separate the effect on muscle and bone from that on other tissues. In trials, ostarine and ligandrol nevertheless lowered HDL cholesterol and the body's own testosterone production, and outside trials several severe cases of liver injury with jaundice are documented. Not a single SARM is approved.

How dangerous are anabolic steroids?

In a Danish registry cohort of 1,189 users, the risk of death was 2.81 times that of men of the same age, the risk of heart muscle disease 8.90 times and of heart attack 3.00 times. The hormone axis shuts down during use and in some men does not fully recover even years later.

Is cardarine a SARM?

No. Cardarine (GW501516) is a PPAR-delta agonist, so it acts on fat metabolism, not on the androgen receptor. Development was stopped in 2006 after a cancer signal appeared in long-term animal studies. GW-0742 is also a PPAR-delta agonist and SR9009 a Rev-erb agonist; neither is a SARM.
