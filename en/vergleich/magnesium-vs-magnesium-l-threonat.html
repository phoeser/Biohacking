Magnesium vs. Magnesium L-Threonate

Magnesium or magnesium L-threonate – is the expensive form worth it?

The short answer

The unique selling point of magnesium L-threonate is the claim that it is the only form that crosses the blood-brain barrier. This claim comes from measurements in the cerebrospinal fluid of rats – in living humans, brain magnesium was not measured in any of the available studies. The three studies, funded by manufacturers or raw material suppliers, run for three to twelve weeks and report better cognitive composite scores and in some cases better sleep; the much-cited cognitive age reduced by 7.5 years is an exploratory analysis, and the two sleep studies contradict each other on the objective readings. Regular magnesium has solid studies on blood pressure and sleep quality and costs a fraction of the price.

Why these numbers

Magnesium

As a cofactor of hundreds of enzymes, very well documented biochemically, with solid studies on blood pressure and sleep quality, but few large outcome trials. Safety well studied; the marketing of individual salt forms goes beyond the comparative data.

Magnesium L-threonate

The entire unique selling point rests on an unconfirmed step: that this form of magnesium brings magnesium into the brain comes from the cerebrospinal fluid of rats – there around 7 percent above baseline and around 16 percent above control on day 24 – and was not measured in living humans in any of the available studies. Three randomized studies with 51, 80 and 100 participants over 3 to 12 weeks report better cognitive composite scores and in some cases better sleep; all three were funded by manufacturers or raw material suppliers, and two were registered only after data collection had ended. The much-cited 7.5 years of cognitive age are explicitly labeled as an exploratory analysis in the original paper, and the two sleep studies contradict each other on the objective readings from the same sleep ring. Human evidence rises from 4 to 5 because there are three RCTs, not two; track record of use rises from 5 to 6 because the substance has been authorized in the EU as a novel food since November 7, 2024 and has been listed in Annex II of Directive 2002/46/EC since November 5, 2025.

Frequently asked questions

Does magnesium L-threonate cross the blood-brain barrier?

In rats, an increased magnesium content was measured in the cerebrospinal fluid. In humans this has never been checked – neither by imaging nor in cerebrospinal fluid. The central advertising claim is therefore unconfirmed in humans.

Which form of magnesium is best?

For most purposes, tolerability decides, not the form. Magnesium oxide is absorbed less well and tends to have a laxative effect; organic compounds such as citrate or glycinate are better tolerated. Comparative studies between the forms with clinical endpoints are rare.

Does magnesium help with sleep?

There are controlled studies with positive results on sleep quality, but the effects are moderate and the studies mostly small. A deficiency should be ruled out beforehand – then the effect is most plausibly explained.
